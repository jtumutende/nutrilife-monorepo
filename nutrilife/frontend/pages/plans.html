Frequently Asked Questions
How does the 7-day free trial work?▼
You get full access to your chosen plan for 7 days completely free. No credit card required to start. After the trial you can pay via MTN MoMo, Airtel Money or VISA.
How do I pay? Do you accept MTN Mobile Money?▼
Yes! We accept MTN Mobile Money, Airtel Money and VISA/Mastercard — all via Pesapal, Uganda's leading payment platform. You'll receive a prompt on your phone for mobile money payments.
Where do you deliver?▼
We currently deliver across Kampala including Nakasero, Kololo, Muyenga, Ntinda, Bugolobi, Kamwokya, Kisimenti, Kabalagala and more. Average delivery time is 30-45 minutes.
Can I cancel my subscription anytime?▼
Yes, you can cancel anytime from your Profile page or by contacting support. There are no cancellation fees. Your access continues until the end of the billing period.
Can I order on WhatsApp?▼
Yes! Our WhatsApp bot at +256 700 123 456 lets you browse the menu, place orders and track deliveries. Just type MENU to get started.
