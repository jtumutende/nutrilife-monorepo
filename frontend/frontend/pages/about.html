Our Mission
Eat Smart.
Live Better.

We believe that good nutrition is the foundation of a healthy life. But in Uganda, access to personalized nutrition advice and healthy prepared meals has always been limited to a few. NutriLife is changing that.

We combine technology, certified nutrition science, and local culinary expertise to deliver affordable, personalized meal plans and fresh food to your door — paid for the way Ugandans pay: with MTN MoMo, Airtel Money, or VISA.
